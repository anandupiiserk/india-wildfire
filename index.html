    India-Centric Forest Fire Early Warning System Prototype

    WRF-Fire simulation setup

    Place up to 5 ignition points on the map of India, set each ignition time in IST, and generate the WRF-Fire namelist.

    Ignition points

    Add up to 5 ignition points. Click the map or type the coordinates, and give each point its own ignition time in IST. Times are converted to UTC for the model.

    Choose the ignition date and time in IST from the calendar.

    0 of 5 ignition points saved. Saved points are listed in the map legend, where you can edit or remove them.

    Model settings

    Three nested domains at 25 km, 5 km and 1 km. Fire is active on the 1 km domain. Domain sizes and positions are fixed for now and will come from the domain wizard later. All other settings are taken from your namelist.

    sr_x and sr_y are always equal. Typing in one freezes the other and copies the value. The model starts 24 h before the first ignition (spin-up) and the ignition times in seconds are worked out automatically. Run length only sets the end date of the model, counted from the last ignition.
